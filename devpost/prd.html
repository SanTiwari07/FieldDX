What we're building

Five changes, on the Niphad demo area

  1. Recording a finding no longer closes the case or marks anything confirmed. The results read Drip line working and Drip line not working. Change
  2. The Investigation updated state replaces the confirmation card: the new evidence, a before-and-after for the causes, and evidence-linked reasons. New
  3. Re-ranking only raises a cause on direct evidence. Nutrient deficiency stays ruled out after Drip line working. Change
  4. The next check updates after a finding, with the reason: leaf undersides for thrips or mildew. New
  5. A state for when none of the current explanations fits well. Built, but not filmed unless a genuine, reproducible example exists. New

Everything else in the core journey already exists and should keep working.

Product decisions, deferred and non-goals

What was decided, and what waits

Product decisions
  • Recording a finding never closes a case. Closing is a deliberate officer action. Ruling out one cause is not the same as confirming another.
  • Ruling out is not confirming. The top cause stays still leading unless field evidence directly confirms it.
  • The finding is named Drip line working (and Drip line not working). It describes the observation, not a diagnosis.
  • Investigation updated replaces the confirmation card. It shows the new evidence, the change in ranking, and the next check.
  • Reversible, never arbitrary. Only direct evidence raises a cause.
  • Quiet update, loud next check.
  • Words, not just colour, for every change.
  • The demo ends at Investigation updated plus the new next check: one reliable, honest investigation from start to finish.
  • When No Cause Fits is built but not filmed unless a genuine, reproducible example exists.
  • Keep the existing design language.
Deferred from the proof of concept
  • Full closing workflow with four outcomes: cause identified, no active issue found, inconclusive / needs follow-up, data or false alarm.
  • Summary and confirmation screen before closing.
  • Follow-up state with scheduling, reminders and tracking.
  • Advanced handling of unusual findings and expert escalation.
  • From scope: accounts, region overview, new areas on demand, field-shape correction, Ask FieldDx chat, phone polish, learning from past findings. These exist in the app but aren't part of the proof.
Non-goals
  • FieldDx does not declare a diagnosis. The officer stays responsible.
  • No celebration or success popup after a finding.
  • No new design system.
  • No new areas for the proof. Niphad only.
