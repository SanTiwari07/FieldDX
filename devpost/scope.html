The unique kernel

FieldDx doesn't stop at a red patch or a list of causes. It picks one practical check whose outcomes best separate the leading causes. The officer's finding then goes back in as evidence and changes the assessment.

"I don't want it to confidently diagnose something when the available data isn't enough."

Who it's for

An FPO field officer

At a farmer producer organisation, starting in Indian farming communities such as Niphad near Nashik. They look after more farms than they can physically visit.

Free satellite maps show them that a field lost greenery, not why. It could be disease, pests, irrigation, harvest or pruning, weather, or a cloudy image. Today they chase alerts with little to go on, or ignore them.

Why it matters

Support judgement, don't replace it

"The gap between detecting a problem and actually helping someone decide what to do about it."

Success means technically useful, honest about its limits, and practical enough that an officer knows what to do next "without blindly trusting an AI-generated answer." Identity: a case file, not a heatmap.

    The proof-of-concept boundary

    Now, later, cut

    "If I were starting again, I'd focus on proving the investigation loop first and add the supporting features only when they became necessary."

    • One real area. The existing Niphad dataset: real Sentinel-2 imagery, field shapes and weather, shipped in the repo.
    • Real alerts, with the image-quality and ordinary-explanation checks.
    • Evidence-backed ranking of causes, with uncertainty shown.
    • One recommended next check per case, chosen to separate the leading causes.
    • Recording a finding, which updates the evidence and the ranking.
    • Phone essentials only: case summary, recommended check and finding form work on a phone.
    Sign-in and accounts Later
    Matter once several officers share the platform. A ready-to-use demo is enough to prove the loop.
    Region overview Later
    Helps officers discover where crop health is changing, but the proof starts once an alert exists for a particular field.
    Marking land and building areas on demand Later
    Important for other regions, but building a new area brings heavy data-processing complexity. Prove the investigation on Niphad first.
    Correcting field shapes Later
    Add it when testing shows that wrong boundaries are a significant problem.
    Ask FieldDx chat Later
    The case screen should already explain the causes, the evidence and the next check, without needing a chat.
    Polishing every screen for phones Later
    Wait until the core workflow is validated.
    Learning from past findings Later
    Over time, findings across cases could make future investigations more useful.
    Nothing is cut permanently. "Most have a genuine purpose; they simply don't all need to exist at the beginning."
    Where this stands

    Written after the build, as a look back

    Most of FieldDx was built before this scope, and the app already includes several Later items. This document names the core the demo has to prove.

    "I think I tried to build too much around the core idea. If I were starting again, I'd focus on proving the investigation loop first."
    Carried into the PRD

    Raised at scope review

    • Define the payoff exactly. After a finding is recorded: how the evidence updates, how the ranking of causes moves, and how the next check responds. The before-and-after should read without a long explanation.
    • Built vs. proposed. Each behaviour says whether FieldDx already does it (verified), or whether it is something to demonstrate or improve, "so we don't accidentally promise behaviour that hasn't been verified."
